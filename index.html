A little page, from my heart

I'm sorry,
my beautiful girl. 💗

I don't want this page to be just an apology. I want it to remind you how deeply special you are to me.

First… I'm really sorry.

I know saying “sorry” cannot magically erase what happened. But I mean it. I am sorry for hurting you, for making you feel bad, and for the moment when my actions didn't match the love and respect you deserve.

You matter to me more than my ego ever should. I don't want to win an argument and lose your smile.

“Your beauty isn't only in your face. It's in the way you care, the way you smile, the little things you do, and the person you are when nobody is watching.”

Do you know how beautiful you are?

You are beautiful when you're happy, when you're excited, when you're being silly, and even on the days when you don't feel beautiful at all.

To me, your smile has its own kind of magic. Your eyes have a story. Your presence can make an ordinary moment feel worth remembering.

And honestly, the most beautiful thing about you is that you are you. There is no replacement for that.

My promise 🤍

🌷 I will think before I speak when emotions get high.
🫶 I will listen instead of simply trying to defend myself.
💗 I will respect your feelings, even when I don't immediately understand them.
🌙 And I won't repeat this mistake knowingly.

One last thing…

I don't expect you to forget everything just because I said sorry. I only hope you can see that I understand my mistake and that I genuinely want to do better.

You deserve love that feels safe, valued, and sincere. And if you give me the chance, I'll show you—not just tell you—that I can be better.

Always yours, with a very sorry heart. 💗
🥺💗🌷

My final message:
Whatever happens, please never forget this — you are genuinely beautiful, deeply precious, and worthy of being loved with patience and respect.

And yes… mujhse dobara aisa nahi hoga. I will try to prove that with my actions.

Made with love, honesty & a little bit of courage ✨
